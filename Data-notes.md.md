\# Data notes

\## GRID3 Nigeria operational wards v3.0

- Source: <https://data.grid3.org/datasets/GRID3::grid3-nga-operational-wards-v3-0/about>
- Downloaded: 15 /9/ 2026
- 9,410 features, polygons
- Columns: ward_name (Ajiya, Alkalawa, Doubeli, Gwadabawa, Jambutu, Karewa, Limawa, Luggere, Nassarawo, Rumde, Yelwa), lga_name (Yola North), state (Adamawa)
- No nulls in ward_name
- Covers my LGA fully

\## OSM roads, extracted via QuickOSM

- Query: highway=\* within Yola North extent
- Extracted: 15/9/2026
- 2,742 features, lines
- Many have no surface tag, so paved and unpaved cannot be separated everywhere
- Coverage looks good in the built-up area, sparse at the edges with less built-up.
